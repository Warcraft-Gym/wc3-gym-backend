# Bundle history

## 2026-10-15

* **Add**: cup announcements. A cup posts in the `announcement_channel_id` channel when it takes signups, an hour before it or a round of it starts, when it or a round is drawn, and its top three when it closes, each pinging the `announcement_role_id` role once. Members take or leave the role with `GET` and `PUT /me/announcements` ([announcement_subscriber](data/tables/announcement_subscriber.md)). [Discord integration](concepts/discord-integration.md#cup-announcements) and [the decision](decisions/cup-announcements.md) state it.
* **Update**: a cup team checks in only once every invited player has accepted: `POST /events/{id}/entrants/{entrant_id}/checkin` and a round's check-in refuse a short team, a runner's check-in of everyone for a round leaves it out, and its `/me/events` row reads `view` with `cup_team.players`. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#team-cups) states it.
* **Add**: the member home's events side. `GET /me/cup-series` lists the series and lobbies the caller still plays in every published cup that is not closed. On `GET /me/events` a player of a cup team reads the cup as joined with `cup_team`, and a cup's row names `team_size`, `ffa` and `custom_game`. A team cup refuses a player's own signup. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#the-member-read) and its [cup series](concepts/events-module.md#cup-series) state it.
* **Add**: custom game cups. `POST /events` for a cup takes `custom_game` with exactly one map: no veto, every game of a series on that map (`fixed` rules, the veto board's fixed map), lobbies on it too, and the pool stays one map ([cup_settings](data/tables/cup_settings.md) `custom_game`). `POST /maps/custom` lets an organizer name a custom map, found or added with the new `custom` mode ([maps](data/tables/maps.md)). The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#cup-series) states it.

## 2026-10-14

* **Add**: team free for all. A team cup's FFA lobby seats cup teams; the lobby read names each seat's team and players, and any player of a seated team enters the places. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#cup-series) states it.
* **Add**: free for all cups play on. A player seated in a lobby enters its places, which must name every seat once from 1; a correction seats the round after again while it is unplayed, and a runner clears a lobby. Every lobby plays a map of the pool (`cup_series.lobby_map_id`, `PUT /cup-series/{id}/lobby-map`), and heats rank on the deepest round reached, so the final decides the top places. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#cup-series) and [cup_series](data/tables/cup_series.md) state it.
* **Add**: a cup's rounds change after creation: rename or move one (`PUT /events/{id}/legs/{leg_id}`), add one after the last (`POST /events/{id}/legs`), remove one not drawn (`DELETE /events/{id}/legs/{leg_id}`). `POST /events` for a cup takes its recurring rule as `recurrence`. [cup_leg](data/tables/cup_leg.md), [cup_recurrence](data/tables/cup_recurrence.md) and the [events module](concepts/events-module.md#cup-rounds) state it.
* **Update**: a seed change after the draw that names a division takes back and draws that division alone; the other divisions keep their series and results. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#entrants-seeds-divisions) states it.
* **Add**: a cup's seeds change after the draw, and the draw follows them: `PUT /events/{id}/stages/{stage_id}/seeds/redraw` writes the seeds and draws the first stage again in one transaction, asking to confirm while its series hold results. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#entrants-seeds-divisions) states it.

## 2026-10-13

* **Add**: recurring cups. A runner sets a rule up from a cup, every week or the nth weekday of the month at a local time ([cup_recurrence](data/tables/cup_recurrence.md)), and makes each next cup on one of the rule's days as a copy of the latest one ([cup_occurrence](data/tables/cup_occurrence.md)) with `POST /cup-recurrences/{id}/occurrences`. `GET /cup-recurrences` and `GET /cup-recurrences/{id}` read the rules, and a cup's full read names its rule as `cup_recurrence`. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#recurring-cups) states it.
* **Add**: a cup can be played over several rounds, each a competition of its own on its own date with its own copy of the cup's stages ([cup_leg](data/tables/cup_leg.md), [cup_leg_stage](data/tables/cup_leg_stage.md)). Players check in per round ([cup_leg_checkin](data/tables/cup_leg_checkin.md)) with `POST` and `DELETE /events/{id}/legs/{leg_id}/checkins`, and a round draws them alone. A runner finishes a round with `POST /events/{id}/legs/{leg_id}/finish`; its places pay points from `cup_settings.points_by_place`, and `GET /events/{id}/overall` adds them up. Closing the cup pays from that table. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#cup-rounds) states it.
* **Add**: a cup chooses at creation whether it splits its players into divisions ([cup_settings](data/tables/cup_settings.md)). A cup without them refuses every division write; a cup with them refuses its draw while a player stands in no division, and its divisions once drawn. A cup's two pins, division and seed, live apart in [cup_entrant_pin](data/tables/cup_entrant_pin.md), set with `PUT /events/{id}/entrants/{entrant_id}/pins`. A seed write may name one division. Hidden seeds hide their source and MMR too. The [events module](concepts/events-module.md#entrants-seeds-divisions) states it.
